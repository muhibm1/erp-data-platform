## What happened

A planned drill. The newest version of three posted invoices was deleted
from `raw.account_move`, as a bad manual fix or a botched restore would. dbt
rebuilt the finance tables, and every dbt check still passed:
`marts_match_odoo_totals` compares the marts with staging, and both read the
same damaged raw table.

The `source_totals` monitor asks Odoo for its own totals, and it failed:
billed $1,529,745.41 in Odoo against $1,505,635.41 in the warehouse.
`edp extract --full` re-read every record in 0.8 seconds, put the missing
versions back, and the totals matched again.

## Lesson

A check that only compares the warehouse with itself cannot see data that
never arrived or went missing. One check must ask the source system. That is
`source_totals`, and it runs after every load.
